TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK HELP
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem 1: "Invalid email or password"
  Solution: Visit /auth-diagnostics, use verified credentials

Problem 2: "System Status: Issue Detected"
  Solution: Click "Refresh Status" button in /auth-diagnostics

Problem 3: "No users found"
  Solution: Click "Reset Data" in /auth-diagnostics

Problem 4: Still not working?
  Solution: Read full LOGIN_RESOLUTION_GUIDE.md (370+ lines)

Problem 5: Need technical details?
  Solution: Read AUTH_FIX_SUMMARY.md and check console logs [v0]
